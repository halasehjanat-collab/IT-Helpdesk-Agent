# KB-017 — IT Service Desk FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. For full procedures follow the linked articles.

**How do I contact the Service Desk?**
Chat with the IT assistant, use the portal `https://help.nakhla.example`, or call ext. 4357. The desk is staffed 08:00-18:00 on business days. Option 1 on the phone line is the 24/7 line for critical incidents.

**Where do visitors get Wi-Fi?**
Reception gives visitors a Nakhla-Guest code. See KB-003.

**How long does a print job wait at the printer?**
FollowMe jobs wait 24 hours, then they are deleted. See the NakhlaPrint quick guide.

**Can I install software myself?**
Use Software Center. Anything else needs a software request (KB-009).

**I left my phone at home. How do I sign in?**
Call the Service Desk and ask for a one-time SMS code. The analyst sends it to the mobile number in your HR record.

**When is a problem a major incident?**
When **5 or more** colleagues are affected by the same problem, the Service Desk treats it as a major incident (P1). Fewer than 5 is handled as a normal ticket.

**My laptop is broken. Can I get a replacement today?**
Ask for a loaner laptop at the Service Desk counter (KB-010).

**Who can see my tickets?**
You, your manager and the IT teams working on them.
